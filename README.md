**Project Proposal: Live Activity for Social Situations**


**Concept**

While watching Apple's WWDC tutorials, I discovered Live Activities, an iOS feature that allows users to access real-time information directly from their Lock Screen or Dynamic Island without opening an app.

I found this feature particularly interesting because it offers a unique way to interact with information without interrupting what you're doing. I wanted to explore how I could use this iOS-native feature in my final project.

My idea is to create an app that provides subtle conversation prompts for different social situations, helping people navigate conversations more naturally and confidently.


**How It Works**

Before entering a social situation, users can select the type of event they are attending, such as a networking event, a party, or a first date.

Based on the selected situation and scheduled time, the app suggests conversation prompts that appear directly through Live Activities on their Lock Screen, functioning like a small, discreet cheat sheet.

The prompts can rotate periodically or be refreshed through a simple interaction, allowing users to access new conversation ideas throughout the event.


**Problem & Opportunity**

In many social situations, especially when meeting new people, it can be difficult to know what to say next. Although looking something up on your phone could help, opening an app and scrolling during a face-to-face conversation can feel distracting or even impolite.

This creates an interesting opportunity for Live Activities. Instead of requiring users to open an app and shift their attention away from the conversation, the interface provides small, glanceable suggestions that are easy to access.


**Design Goal**

My goal is not to script conversations or tell people exactly what to say, but to provide gentle prompts that help them feel more prepared and confident in different social settings.

Through this project, I want to explore how Live Activities can support real-world social interactions, creating a lightweight digital experience that stays in the background while encouraging people to engage more with those around them.
